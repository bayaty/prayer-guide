# Privacy Policy

**Prayer Guide**
Last updated: 30 August 2026

## The short version

Prayer Guide collects nothing. There is no account, no sign in, no analytics,
no advertising, and no network connection. Nothing you do in the app leaves
your device.

## What the app stores

Your settings are saved on your device so the app opens the way you left it:

- whether recommended steps are shown alongside the required ones
- whether you are following wudu or tayammum
- whether Arabic, transliteration and translation are shown
- whether prayers open as one long scroll or one step at a time

These are stored locally using the operating system's standard preference
storage. They are never transmitted anywhere, and they are removed when you
uninstall the app.

## What the app does not do

- No personal information is collected
- No usage or analytics data is gathered
- No advertising or tracking identifiers are used
- No data is shared with third parties, because none is collected
- No account or email address is required
- No internet connection is used

## Location

Prayer Guide does not request or use your device location. The qibla compass
works from a city you choose from a list, and the direction is calculated on
your device using publicly known coordinates.

## Children

The app is rated 4+ and is safe for all ages. Since it collects no data at
all, it collects no data from children.
